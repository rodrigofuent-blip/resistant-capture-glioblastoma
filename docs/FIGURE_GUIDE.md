# Manuscript figure index

The `results/figures/main/` and `results/figures/supplementary/` directories contain the figure files corresponding to the numbered illustrations. The mapping and data sources are listed in `FIGURE_MAP.csv` and `MANUSCRIPT_CROSS_REFERENCE.md`.

Main Figure 5 is a vector copy of the conceptual diagram from the typeset manuscript (page 26). The PDF in this repository reproduces the diagram but is not an editable drawing-source file. Conceptual diagrams are distinguished from graphs generated by numerical scripts.

Supplementary Figure S4 is the event-localized 25×25 diagnostic with four panels. Additional figures under `results/diagnostics/` are numerical convergence and sensitivity views, not numbered illustrations in the manuscripts.
